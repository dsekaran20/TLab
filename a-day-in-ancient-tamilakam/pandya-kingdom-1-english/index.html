This is historical fiction set roughly around 30 BCE, in the Pandyan world of ancient Tamilakam—about 2,000 years ago. Dating Sangam texts is uncertain and debated. Puranānūru is generally placed broadly in the early historic/Sangam period, often roughly between the last centuries BCE and the early centuries CE; this story uses the poem as a literary thread rather than claiming it was certainly composed in exactly 30 BCE. The Tamil poem is Puranānūru 192, attributed to Kaṇiyaṉ Pūṅkuṉṟaṉār.
