The detailed panel distinguishes what is broadly established from literary tradition. Many early Tamil works were transmitted orally and in manuscript culture, so authorship, location, and exact dates may be uncertain.

Method note: “Associated places” identify locations prominent in a text, author tradition, or later preservation—not necessarily a proven place of composition. Political labels are broad historical context, not evidence that a ruler commissioned the work. For collective anthologies, “author” means the surviving traditional attributions, not a complete modern biographical record.
