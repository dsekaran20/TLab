Tamil Lab

Personal interactive projects in history, language, culture, learning, and technology. I created Tamil Lab as a place to follow my curiosity through interactive projects. I am interested in ancient history, Tamil literature and culture, and the ways stories can make learning more engaging. Through timelines, small games, and storytelling prototypes, I am exploring how people, places, and ideas connect across time. These projects are works in progress: designed to make complex subjects more approachable, invite curiosity, and improve through research and feedback.

5000 years of World and India Timeline

A historical timeline showing overlapping civilizations, religions, empires and dynasties across time.

Tamil Literature Timeline

An interactive chronology of selected Tamil texts, authors, places, political settings, and literary traditions.

The Pearl and the Poem

A short interactive historical-fiction journey from Korkai to Madurai, inspired by Sangam-era Tamilakam.
